MOUNTLIST ↻

Your Mac sees FujiNet on its external floppy port as one 800K floppy drive plus up to four HD20 hard disks. Pick an image on a host, then choose which slot it goes in.

Hard disks HD20 · slots 1–4

.dsk HFS/MFS volume or drive image · .image DiskCopy 4.2 · .sit .sea .hqx .bin archives · up to 32 MB (65,535 blocks). Put your startup volume in slot 1.

Floppy slot 5

.moof · 400K/800K .dsk .img · .image · archives holding a floppy. Mount R/W to let the Mac write.

Loads every slot from the saved config. The Mac has no CONFIG program, so after a FujiNet restart nothing is loaded until you press this (or turn off Boot CONFIG).

Slots 6–8 aren't connected to the Mac: the floppy port only addresses four HD20s and one floppy drive.

Before you swap disks
  1. HD20: shut the Mac down, or drag the volume to the Trash, before you eject or replace it here. The Mac caches the volume header and writes it into whatever image is in the slot next, which then shows up as damaged.
  2. Floppy: eject on the Mac first (drag it to the Trash), then mount the next image here. Ejecting here while the Mac still shows the disk gets the two sides out of step.
  3. Booting from a floppy: Restart and Shut Down eject every floppy. Power the Mac off, mount the image, then power it on.
  4. Archives and floppies share PSRAM. Mount a large archive before the floppy.
  5. R/W mode lets the Mac write. The file on the host must be writable by the server too.
  6. HD20 support is in the 128K ROM (512Ke, Plus, SE, SE/30…). A 64K-ROM 128K/512K needs the Hard Disk 20 startup file on a floppy.
